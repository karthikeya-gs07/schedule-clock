CalenTime Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

CalenTime shows your meetings as arcs on your Wear OS watch face. It has a phone app and a watch app. This page explains what they read, where it goes, and what they never do.

The short version

  • CalenTime reads your calendar so it can show your meetings on your own watch.
  • Your calendar goes only from your phone to your watch. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • The apps have no internet access, no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

What the apps read

  • Calendar events (with your permission): for the calendars you switch on in the app, from yesterday to the end of tomorrow, each event's start and end time, title, location, whether it's all-day, and whether it's marked private. Declined and cancelled events are skipped.
  • Your calendar list: the names, colours and account names of the calendars on your phone, so you can choose which ones to use.
  • Your watch's connection: whether a watch is paired and whether the CalenTime watch face is on it.

Where it goes

The phone app sends the events to the CalenTime app on your paired watch, using Wear OS's built-in phone-to-watch connection (provided by Google Play services). When your phone and watch can't reach each other directly, Google Play services may carry this through Google's servers, encrypted, between your own devices. We (the developer) never receive it.

Private events are sent without their title or location: the watch only learns that you're busy.

The watch app may also read the calendar stored on the watch itself, if you allow it, for the same purpose.

What is stored

The phone app keeps your settings (which calendars, colours and meeting buzzes you chose) on your phone. The watch app keeps the latest events and settings on your watch so the face can draw them. Nothing is stored anywhere else. Uninstalling the apps deletes all of it.

What the apps never do

  • No internet access: the phone app doesn't even have the internet permission.
  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising, tracking, analytics or crash reporting.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.

Permissions

  • Calendar (phone and watch): to read your meetings, as above.
  • Install and set watch faces (watch): to put the CalenTime face on your watch and, once, make it your watch face. You're asked first.
  • Alarms and vibration (watch): to buzz before, during and near the end of each meeting. You can turn each buzz off in the phone app.

Children

CalenTime isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date above changed.
